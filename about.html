About Savannah Outdoor

Strength. Reliability. Visibility.

Our story

Savannah Outdoor is a premium Sri Lankan branding and advertising company focused on high-impact visual branding for businesses that want real market presence.

We specialize in outdoor, indoor, and vehicle branding — combining modern advertising design with industrial-grade fabrication and expert installation.

Mission

“To create impactful branding solutions that make businesses impossible to ignore.”

Vision

To become Sri Lanka’s most trusted name in premium large-scale branding and visual advertising.

Brand identity

Our visual language uses a bold black and orange theme inspired by the African savannah landscape.

The elephant

Our symbol represents strength, reliability, dominance, large-scale presence, and powerful visibility.

How we feel

Bold, premium, industrial, cinematic, modern, and powerful — built for brands that refuse to blend in.

What we’re not

We’re not a quick print shop. We’re a full-scale branding execution partner from concept to installation.

What makes us different

Integrated delivery

Design, fabrication, and installation under one roof — fewer handoffs, faster delivery, tighter quality control.

Built for the real world

We engineer for visibility and durability — materials and finishing that hold up outdoors and in high-traffic spaces.

Island-wide coverage

Professional teams and project coordination across Sri Lanka for consistent execution wherever you operate.

Creative + industrial

Modern advertising visuals backed by fabrication expertise — premium agency craft with production muscle.
